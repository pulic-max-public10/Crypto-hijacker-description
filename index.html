Attack Vectors

🕳️

Wallet Drainers

Phishing sites or malicious smart contracts that trick you into signing a transaction granting the attacker permission to empty your wallet.

🕵️

Infostealers

Malware that locates and exfiltrates your private keys, seed phrases, or wallet .dat files — giving the attacker permanent account control.

⛏️

Cryptojacking

Your device's CPU/GPU is secretly hijacked to mine crypto for the attacker, draining battery and performance without your knowledge.
